About me

I'm a computer science student at UNBC (Dean's List, class of 2027) who likes building things that leave the screen — software that ends up in someone's hands, not just in a repo.

At the Social Robotics Lab I write the Python and ROS 2 that lets robots run on their own in the real world. In my coursework I've optimized scheduling across 27,000+ tasks with graph algorithms and shipped full-stack apps with Angular, Node, and MySQL.

Outside of school I supervise a team of 15–20 at The Home Depot — which taught me more about communication, ownership, and staying calm under pressure than any textbook.

Screen-Free Robot-Integrated Learning Games

2026 — present

Five fully hardware-based games — Memory Matrix, Match Point, Scramble Word, Misty Maze, and Tangram — built on Raspberry Pi with touch, RFID, buzzers, AI object detection, and ArUco markers.
